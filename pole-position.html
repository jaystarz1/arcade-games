Pole Position

A tribute to Pole Position (Namco, 1982) and Pole Position II (1983) in one cabinet. Qualify for the grid, then race the clock — LO gear off the line, HI for the straights. Touch a rival and you're a fireball. Course 1 is the original Fuji; courses 2–4 are the sequel's Test, Suzuka and Seaside runs. Best laps and top scores are saved.
