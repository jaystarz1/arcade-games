A tribute to After Burner II (Sega AM2, 1987) — the X-Board sprite-scaler in the rotating deluxe cockpit. Sweep the reticle across the bandits, hammer the missile button, and when the klaxon sounds: change speed — a locked missile can't follow a throttle change. 23 stages, refuelling every few blocks. High score is saved.
